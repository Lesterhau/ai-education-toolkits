## What's in them

Each toolkit includes: a named AI Curriculum Integration
Lead with a 30-day action plan, a three-zone student AI
use policy (AI-Free, AI-Transparent, AI-Collaborative),
student data privacy and vendor requirements including
written FERPA confirmation, an AI-Free Declaration Track
for students with principled ethical objections,
succession planning, and Texas-specific political risk
analysis.
